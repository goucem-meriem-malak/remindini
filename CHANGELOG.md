# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-19
First release.
- Subscription list with price, cycle, next charge, category, notes.
- Free-trial tracking with a "cancel before you are charged" reminder.
- Local reminders (N days before each charge, at a time you choose).
- Home, Calendar, Insights (by category, top subscriptions, saved by cancelling), Settings.
- Pause, resume, mark as cancelled, delete with undo.
- English, French, Arabic (full RTL). Light and dark themes.
- Offline only: data stays on the phone. Backup and restore through the clipboard.
- Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (drafts) inside the app, acceptance version stored locally.
